TusAnkiM Support

For technical issues, feedback, card corrections, or purchase access, open a support request. Do not include personal health data, passwords, or payment-card details.

Purchases

Apple processes payments. If a previously purchased catalog is locked, use “Restore Purchase” inside the app. For billing or refunds, use Apple’s Report a Problem service.
