# Code of conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to making participation in the TusAnkiM community a respectful, harassment-free
experience for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience level, education, or
personal characteristics.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, constructive, and specific.
- Assume good intent while addressing the technical substance of a disagreement.
- Welcome questions and help others learn.
- Respect privacy and never publish another person's study data or health information.
- Accept responsibility, apologize when appropriate, and learn from mistakes.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, personal attacks, deliberate disruption,
doxxing, and publishing private information without permission are not tolerated.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through their GitHub profile. Reports
will be reviewed fairly and kept confidential as far as reasonably possible. Maintainers may edit
or remove content and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when necessary.

This code applies in repository spaces and when someone is publicly representing the project.
